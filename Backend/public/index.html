Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At OnlyStudents ("we", "our", "us"), we prioritize your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when using the OnlyStudents mobile app and platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Info: Name, student email address, campus affiliation, and profile data.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, and interaction data to improve platform experience.
  • Payment Metadata: Transaction IDs and subscription status processed securely via Razorpay. We do not store raw card numbers or UPI PINs.

2. How We Use Information

To provide and improve our student community platform, manage subscriptions, verify campus identities, and ensure compliance with RBI and applicable regulatory requirements.

3. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices. Payment data is handled exclusively by Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers (e.g., Razorpay for payments) as required to operate the platform.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You retain access to premium features until the billing period ends.

Refund requests made within 7 days of initial subscription purchase are eligible for a full refund, provided premium features have not been substantially used.

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method via Razorpay within 5–7 business days.
