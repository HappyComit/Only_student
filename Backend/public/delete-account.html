Account & Personal Data Deletion Request

Developer: OnlyStudents Team (CampusHive)

At OnlyStudents, we respect your privacy and provide transparent control over your personal account and data. If you wish to delete your account and associated data, please follow the steps outlined below.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Option 1: Contact Support via Email

Send an email from your registered student email address to our support team:

✉️ Support Email: onlystudentshelps@gmail.com

Subject Line: Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email/Username]

Our team processes deletion requests within 48 hours.

Types of Data Deleted Upon Request

When your account deletion request is processed, the following data is permanently erased from our active servers:

  • User Profile Data: Full name, student username, email address, phone number, university name, department, academic year, profile photo, and bio.
  • Listings & Gigs: All active service listings, posted gigs, and promotional descriptions.
  • Chat Messages: All in-app chat history and communications between buyers and sellers.
  • Preferences & Notifications: Push tokens, app settings, and saved preferences.

Data Retained for Legal & Regulatory Compliance

Certain limited transactional records may be retained as mandated by law:

  • Payment & Tax Records: Booking fee order metadata processed via Razorpay is retained for compliance with financial accounting standards and statutory regulations in India (RBI/Tax compliance guidelines).
