Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

1. Introduction

OnlyStudents ("CampusHive") operates a peer-to-peer campus marketplace platform for university students. We are committed to protecting your privacy and security.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Email, username, name, phone number, university, department, and year.
  • Profile Information: Profile photo, bio, skills, and optional payment UPI IDs.
  • Payment Info: Transaction metadata processed securely via Razorpay.
  • Chat Messages: In-app communications between buyers and sellers.

3. Data Usage & Security

All data transmitted between the app and servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. We do not sell user data to third parties. Financial transactions are securely handled by PCI-DSS compliant Razorpay servers.

4. Account & Data Deletion

Users can request full deletion of their account and associated data by emailing onlystudentshelps@gmail.com or visiting our Account Deletion Page.
